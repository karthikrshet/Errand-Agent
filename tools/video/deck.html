Five guarantees, not five hopes

✅

No action without approval

Single-use grant, fingerprinted to provider, slot and price. Ambiguous replies never grant.

🧾

No double booking

Write-ahead intent, idempotency key, unique constraints, atomic grant + intent.

🔍

Booked only if verified

On timeout, ask get_booking_status first. Claims checked against the ledger.

🛡️

Injection can't act

Tool-less sub-agent reads reviews. Per-state tool allowlist. Grants only from the user.

🧠

Forgetting is real

Value scrubbed from memory, history and live requests. The manifest proves it.
